AgilA32

  • AgilA32 simulator (Developed by Alexander Co Abad with the help of Claude AI (Sonnet 5.5))

    The chip's Verilog compiled to WebAssembly. Run the boot ROM, the PIO tests and your own programs in the browser, with pins, waveforms and CPU/PIO state. Nothing to install.

    RV32I CPU with an RP2040-compatible PIO block (two state machines), for Tiny Tapeout on IHP SG13G2.

  • AgilA32_FPGA_core

    FPGA self-test of the RV32I core for the Tiny Tapeout FPGA breakout.
