CAN bus tutorial

An interactive introduction to the Controller Area Network, in the same style as the ReRAM and PCM lessons. The lesson plan runs from the wired bus, through one message on it, to a whole sensor network, with a reference page for the equations and terms.

Lessons

Step 1 of 3  CAN bus vs separate wires: the wired-AND bus, differential signalling, bit timing and stuffing.

Step 2 of 3  How does a message win the bus?: frame fields, CRC, bit stuffing, arbitration, acknowledgement and error counters.

Step 3 of 3  Six sensors and a dashboard: wheel speed, engine speed, throttle, steering angle, coolant temperature and fuel level send CAN frames to a dashboard. Add extra traffic or noise and watch the readings go stale.

Reference  CAN reference: equations with worked examples, a searchable glossary, symbols and units, what every setting does, and further reading.

Developed by Alexander Co Abad with the help of Claude AI (Sonnet 5.5). A teaching model, not a model of a specific device.
