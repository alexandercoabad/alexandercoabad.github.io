PCM compute-in-memory

A three-step lesson on phase-change memory, mirroring the ReRAM series. Each page is self-contained and runs in the browser.

  1. Step 1: PCM vs ReRAM

    Send the same pulses to a PCM cell and a ReRAM cell, compare their I-V curves, and see drift, variation and wear.

  2. Step 2: How a weight gets written into a cell

    Follow one weight from a number to a conductance: RESET, then SET pulses with read-back, and what happens when a pulse overshoots.

  3. Step 3: PCM crossbar simulator

    Matrix-vector multiply with Ohm's and Kirchhoff's laws, including the PCM write model and conductance drift after the write.

  4. Compute-in-memory vs Von Neumann

    How much compute a PCM crossbar delivers compared with a processor that fetches every weight: speed, energy, TOPS and TOPS per watt, with scenarios from the processor's worst case to its best. Read after Step 3.

  5. PCM reference

    Equations, glossary, symbols and settings for all three steps.

Developed by Alexander Co Abad with the help of Claude AI (Sonnet 5.5). A teaching model, not a model of a specific device.
